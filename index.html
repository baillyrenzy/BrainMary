Daily Wellness

Habits that help your brain work at its best

A capsule is one piece of the puzzle. These everyday habits are widely recommended for brain health, and they pair well with a supplement routine.

Sleep on schedule

Most adults do best with seven to nine hours. A steady bedtime helps memory settle and focus return.

Move every day

A brisk 30-minute walk supports blood flow, mood and energy.

Eat for your brain

Favor vegetables, berries, nuts, whole grains, olive oil and oily fish. Limit ultra-processed food.

Drink enough water

Even mild dehydration can make it harder to concentrate.

Keep learning

Pick up a language, an instrument or a puzzle. New challenges keep the mind active.

Stay connected

Regular conversation and time with friends and family are good for mood and mental sharpness.

Manage stress

Breathing exercises, time outdoors and short breaks lower the mental load.

Take screen breaks

Every hour, look away, stand up and stretch for a minute or two.

Brain-friendly foods to enjoy

A balanced plate is the foundation of any brain-health routine.

Leafy greens

Spinach, kale and rocket are rich in vitamins and plant compounds.

Berries

Blueberries, strawberries and blackcurrants are known for their antioxidants.

Nuts and seeds

Walnuts, almonds and pumpkin seeds make an easy, filling snack.

Oily fish

Salmon, sardines and mackerel are staples of the Mediterranean diet.

Whole grains

Oats, brown rice and quinoa give steady energy through the day.

Herbs and spices

Rosemary, turmeric and sage add flavour without extra salt.

A simple day with BrainMary

  • Morning: Take your capsules with breakfast and a glass of water, as directed on the label.
  • Midday: Walk after lunch and tackle your hardest task while energy is high.
  • Afternoon: Hydrate, snack on nuts or fruit and take a short screen break.
  • Evening: Switch off screens early, read and keep a regular bedtime.

Five-minute brain breaks

Quick resets for when focus starts to slip.

  1. Breathe

    Inhale for four counts, hold for four and exhale for six. Repeat five times.

  2. Stretch

    Roll your shoulders, stretch your neck and stand tall to release tension.

  3. Step outside

    Natural light and fresh air are a quick lift for mood and alertness.

  4. Refill your water

    Drink a full glass before you return to your task.

Evening wind-down checklist

Better evenings make sharper mornings.

  • Stop work and put screens away an hour before bed.
  • Keep your bedroom cool, dark and quiet.
  • Write tomorrow's to-do list so your mind can switch off.
  • Avoid heavy meals and caffeine late in the day.
  • Read, stretch or listen to calm music.
